Where the Loop Closes

Two procedures, each built from three runs on video. They perform the same steps in the same order. Only one of them acts on what it finds.

The difference, in four stages

Both procedures throw away material that can’t become a slice. Only one throws away a slice that came out wrong. The instruction for discarding an unusable remnant is identical in both. What differs is what happens to a finished slice judged too thin: one culls it, the other notes it and keeps cutting the same way. Measurement that changes nothing isn’t control, and an open loop is what produces run-to-run variation in width.
